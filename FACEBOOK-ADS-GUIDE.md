# SongGift Facebook Ads — Complete Step-by-Step Guide

> **What you need before starting:**
> - Facebook account with a SongGift Facebook Page already created
> - A payment method (credit/debit card)
> - Your files:
>   - `banner.png` (image ad)
>   - `website AD.mp4` (video ad — landscape/square)
>   - `website AD- tiktok.mp4` (video ad — vertical/reels)

---

# PART 1: SET UP META ADS MANAGER (One-Time Setup)

### Step 1: Go to Meta Ads Manager
1. Open your browser
2. Go to **https://adsmanager.facebook.com**
3. Log in with the Facebook account that owns the SongGift page
4. If it asks you to create an Ads account, click **"Create Account"**

### Step 2: Add Payment Method
1. Click the **⚙️ gear icon** (bottom-left corner) or go to **Business Settings**
2. Click **"Payment Settings"** in the left sidebar
3. Click **"Add Payment Method"**
4. Enter your card info
5. Click **"Save"**

### Step 3: Verify Your Domain (Important for tracking)
1. In Ads Manager, click the **☰ hamburger menu** (top left)
2. Click **"Events Manager"**
3. Click **"Add Event"** → **"From a new website"**
4. Select **"Meta Pixel"**
5. Name it: `SongGift Pixel`
6. Enter your website: `https://songgift.app`
7. Click **"Continue"**
8. It will give you a pixel code — **we can add this later if needed**
9. For now, just click **"Done"** to create the pixel

# PART 2: CREATE YOUR FIRST CAMPAIGN

### Step 4: Start a New Campaign
1. Go to **https://adsmanager.facebook.com**
2. Click the green **"+ Create"** button (top left)
3. A popup appears: **"Choose a campaign objective"**

### Step 5: Choose Campaign Objective
1. You'll see 6 options. Click **"Sales"**
   - *(This tells Facebook to show your ad to people most likely to buy)*
2. Click **"Continue"**

### Step 6: Name Your Campaign
1. **Campaign Name:** `SongGift — Conversions — Broad`
2. Leave these settings as-is:
   - **Special Ad Categories:** Leave OFF (unless you're in a restricted category — gifts are not)
   - **Buying Type:** Auction *(default)*
3. **Campaign Budget:** Toggle ON **"Advantage Campaign Budget"**
4. Set **Daily Budget:** `$20` *(start here, scale up once you see results)*
5. Click **"Next"**

---

### Step 7: Set Up the Ad Set (Targeting)
This is where you tell Facebook WHO to show your ads to.

1. **Ad Set Name:** `US — 25-55 — Gift Givers — Broad`

2. **Conversion Location:** Select **"Website"**

3. **Pixel:** Select your **"SongGift Pixel"** *(if you set one up)*
   - If no pixel, select **"Link Clicks"** as optimization for now

4. **Performance Goal:** Select **"Maximize number of conversions"**

5. **Conversion Event:** Select **"Purchase"** *(if pixel is set up)* or **"Link Clicks"** *(if not)*

# PART 5: WHAT TO EXPECT

## First 3-5 Days
- Facebook is in **"learning phase"** — it's testing who to show your ads to
- **Don't touch anything** during this time
- Costs per click may be higher — this is normal
- You need ~50 events (clicks/purchases) for learning to complete

## Metrics to Watch
| Metric | Good Range | Where to Find |
|---|---|---|
| **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** | 1%+ | Ads Manager → Columns → Performance |
| **CPC (Cost Per Click)** | Under $2 | Ads Manager → Columns → Performance |
| **CPM (Cost Per 1K Impressions)** | $5–$25 | Ads Manager → Columns → Performance |
| **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** | 2x+ | Once you have purchases tracked |

## After 5-7 Days
- Turn OFF the worst-performing ad (lowest CTR)
- Increase budget by 20% on the winning ad
- Never increase budget by more than 20% at a time
